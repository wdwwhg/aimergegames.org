# About AI Merge Games

_Last updated: October 16, 2026_

AI Merge Games is an independent playground for original 2D browser game mashups. We explore a simple question: what happens when two gameplay ideas meet?

## What you can use today

Moss Knight, Portal Hop and Meadow Survivor are handcrafted, playable examples. They are free to play without an account. Their geometric artwork and game logic were made for this project.

## What is coming

We are building a generator that creates original browser games from two gameplay inspirations and a short description. Live AI generation and credit purchases are not open yet. Generated games will be private by default.

## Our editorial approach

We distinguish planned features, premade examples and independently verified behavior. Third-party tool descriptions include source notes and review dates. A link to a provider is not a hands-on endorsement or a security audit.

We are not affiliated with the publishers of games named as inspirations. We do not redistribute their game files or third-party mod installers.

## Contact

For account-related questions, use the support section in your account settings. No public support email has been configured for this local version.
