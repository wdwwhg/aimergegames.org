# Credits and refunds

_Last updated: October 16, 2026_

## Purchases are closed

No live credit packs, prices or subscriptions are offered. The price page describes a planned credit system. Administrator-only Stripe sandbox transactions do not collect real money.

## Planned generation usage

One new game generation consumes one generation credit and includes at most one automatic repair attempt. Playing, restarting or downloading a saved game does not consume another credit.

If a generation finishes in a failed or timed-out state, its consumed credit is returned once. This is a credit restoration, not a payment refund. A successful startup check does not guarantee game balance or complete gameplay quality.

## Paid-pack refunds

Eligibility, request windows and expiry rules have not been set because no paid packs are on sale. These terms must be published alongside the final products before real purchases open.

## Contact

Use the support section in account settings for order or credit questions. A public support contact must be configured before launch.
